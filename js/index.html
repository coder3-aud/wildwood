YOUR FIELD GUIDE

How to Play

Wildwood · Field Test 01
A little world to explore.

Movement

  • WASD or the arrow keys to move.
  • SPACE to jump (⅛ stamina). Jump to reach flying enemies.
  • SHIFT to dash (¼ stamina).

Combat and gathering

  • Left-click to attack in the direction you are moving or last moved, or eat selected food.
  • With the sword, hold E and left-click to strike one block up (½ stamina), one-shot airborne enemies there, and knock back and stun enemies within three blocks.
  • Hold E to raise your shield toward the mouse and block attacks from the front.
  • Right-click rocks to mine them; right-click the ground to build.
  • Walk over rare drops to collect them. Number keys 1–8 select your inventory.
  • Three matching brew actions grant a boost: double damage (3s), invincibility (5s), or infinite stamina (10s).

Your world

  • Use Save / Load to keep your world in one of three slots.
  • Press P to open the map editor. Click or drag on the map to paint; each selected sprite fills one 32 × 32 tile.
  • Press ESC to pause or resume.

✦ The world is built from editable tiles — ready for custom sprites and new game features.

Tree spawning

Chance values are shares of object spawns; the remaining spawns are rocks or bushes. Trees use the green design 80% of the time and orange 20%. Each tree spans 2 × 3 tiles; only the bottom two tiles are solid.
